# Evaluation goal

Evaluate the importance of content for a Chinese operations/growth professional tracking how AI is being applied in real-world user growth, marketing, and product scenarios. The reader wants actionable insights, not academic theory.

# Scoring rubric

- **9-10: Must-read.** Landmark AI+growth case studies with published metrics (e.g., +30% conversion, 2x retention), a replicable playbook, and clear before/after data. Major AI product launches that meaningfully change growth channel economics.
- **7-8: Highly valuable.** Strong case studies or experiments with some data, novel AI applications in recognizable growth contexts, practical frameworks for AI-powered growth workflows, or tools that demonstrably lower the barrier for growth teams.
- **5-6: Worth noting.** Useful observations, early experiments without full data, emerging patterns, thoughtful commentary on AI's role in growth, or incremental AI features in growth/marketing tools. Has a clear takeaway even if not fully validated.
- **3-4: Borderline relevant.** Content touches AI and growth but lacks depth — brief mentions, product announcements without data, generic advice, or routine updates from growth-related sources. May have a single useful nugget.
- **0-2: Skip.** Pure AI release notes without growth angle, growth content with no AI connection, promotional material, or content entirely outside the AI×growth intersection.

# Evaluation guidance

Reward: concrete metrics (retention, conversion, CAC, LTV, DAU), named tools and techniques, real company examples, replicable tactics, and cross-domain insight (AI capability → growth outcome).

Penalize: vague claims ("AI-powered," "revolutionary"), generic advice, missing data, promotion-heavy tone, and content that covers AI or growth alone without connecting the two.

Consider whether a growth/operations practitioner could act on this information within their workflow. The best content bridges the gap between "AI can do X" and "here's how to use X to improve metric Y."
