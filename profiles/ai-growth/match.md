# AI × Growth profile

Use this profile for content at the intersection of artificial intelligence and user/customer growth — where AI is the tool and growth is the outcome.

Typical items include:
- AI tools or techniques applied to user acquisition, activation, retention, referral, or monetization (AARRR)
- Case studies of AI-driven growth experiments with concrete metrics and results
- Product launches where AI is the core differentiator driving adoption and growth
- Practical guides on using AI for content marketing, SEO, paid ads optimization, email personalization, or social media strategy
- AI-powered analytics, A/B testing, or user behavior insights that inform growth decisions
- Industry analysis of how AI is reshaping customer acquisition channels and costs
- Open-source AI tools or platforms that lower the barrier for growth teams

Do not use this profile for:
- Pure AI research papers without application context
- Generic AI product release notes without growth implications
- Growth or marketing theory with no AI component
- Pure technical infrastructure content (unless it directly enables growth use cases)
- Financial market analysis or stock coverage
