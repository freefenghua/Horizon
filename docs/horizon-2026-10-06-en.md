### [AI Short Drama Overseas: 221,900 Titles, Under 0.5% Break 100M Views](https://www.woshipm.com/ai/6473856.html) ⭐️ 7.0/10

A review of 30 AI short-drama products going overseas, drawing on DataEye, Sensor Tower, Diandian Data and other sources, shows that in H1 2026 overseas micro-drama downloads grew 79% year over year while in-app purchase revenue grew only 13%. AI has mainly landed in translation/dubbing, creative assets and ad buying rather than full episode generation: dubbing cost per title fell to RMB 300–500 \(over 80% below manual dubbing\), and per-minute AI live-action drama cost dropped from RMB 4,000–5,000 in 2025 to about RMB 1,000 in 2026. Despite this, of 221,900 AI dramas and anime dramas newly launched on Douyin in H1 2026, only 1,055 surpassed 100 million views \(0.47%\), and fewer than 1.3% reached the 50-million-view break-even threshold. Ad buying accounts for roughly 50% of total overseas cost, and 80–90% of overseas projects fail to recoup costs, with the top three platforms \(DramaBox, ReelShort, NetShort\) taking nearly half of TOP20 in-app purchase revenue. The source notes the original article was truncated, so some arguments are incomplete.

**「Takeaway」** For AI content growth, treat AI as a cost and speed lever for translation, asset production and ad buying first, and validate unit economics against the 50-million-view break-even line before scaling output, since cheaper production alone does not lift recoup rates when ad buying dominates cost.

### [Tencent&\#x27;s Knocket: A Free AI Receptionist for Small Sites](https://www.woshipm.com/ai/6473812.html) ⭐️ 5.0/10

Tencent has quietly launched Knocket, a free AI receptionist that lets site owners embed a chat entry point, upload FAQs or documents for AI auto-replies, and escalate to humans when needed. The tool addresses a common pain point for indie developers and small products: visitors who want to ask about pricing, features, or partnerships have no lightweight way to reach the owner, since email is slow, WeChat has restrictions, and full customer-service systems are costly. Knocket offers three entry points — a Web Widget \(a script pasted into WordPress, Shopify, or plain HTML\), a Contact Page \(a knocket.ai link usable in bios, email signatures, or QR codes\), and a Mobile Widget for iOS, Android, Flutter, and React Native apps — all feeding into a shared Inbox with Telegram and email notifications. The AI Agent reads uploaded knowledge \(FAQ, web pages, PDF/TXT/DOC/DOCX/HTML/MD/CSV/XLSX files up to 10MB each\) and answers first, with 218+ preset handoff trigger phrases across dozens of languages; it supports 50 languages and is not locked to Tencent&\#x27;s own model, allowing OpenAI Chat Completions-compatible API keys. The source is a product introduction with no reported metrics on conversion, retention, or engagement, so its growth impact remains unverified.

rss · 人人都是产品经理 · Oct 6, 03:01

**「AI Technique」** Knocket uses a retrieval-augmented generation \(RAG\) setup: owners upload FAQs, web pages, or documents \(PDF, TXT, DOC, DOCX, HTML, MD, CSV, XLSX, up to 10MB each\) into a knowledge base, and the AI Agent answers visitor questions by referencing that indexed content. It is model-agnostic, accepting an API Key, Endpoint, and Model ID via an OpenAI Chat Completions-compatible interface, and it uses 218+ preset multilingual trigger phrases plus model understanding to decide when to hand off to a human.

**「Growth Impact」** The source reports no measurable growth outcomes — no conversion lift, retention improvement, or CAC reduction data is provided. The implied mechanism is that a lightweight, always-on chat entry point reduces missed inquiries from visitors who would otherwise leave rather than fill out a form or wait for email, but this remains an unverified product claim rather than a demonstrated result.

**「Takeaway」** For a small site or indie product without a support team, test a free AI receptionist by uploading your existing FAQ and pricing docs, then measure whether it captures inquiries that previously went unanswered — but treat the vendor&\#x27;s &\#x27;always-on&\#x27; promise as a hypothesis to validate with your own reply-rate and conversion data.
