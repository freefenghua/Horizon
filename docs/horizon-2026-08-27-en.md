### [Zhao Yiming&\#x27;s Offline Growth Algorithm: Interception Over Location](https://www.woshipm.com/marketing/6455238.html) ⭐️ 7.0/10

This article analyzes Zhao Yiming&\#x27;s offline growth strategy, revealing that its success is not merely due to low prices but a systematic &\#x27;interception&\#x27; model that pre-embeds stores along target customers&\#x27; daily routes \(e.g., near schools, communities, and subway stations\) rather than relying on traditional location selection. The company, part of the Mingming Henmang Group, grew from zero to over 30,000 signed stores in less than eight years, capturing over 75% of the market share together with Wanchen Group. Key operational metrics include an inventory turnover of 11.6 days, a monthly repurchase rate of 6.2 times for community stores, and annual sales per square meter of 3,000 yuan. The article also highlights the structural conflict between the brand&\#x27;s expansion goals and franchisee profitability, as new stores cannibalize existing ones. For growth practitioners, the case demonstrates a replicable model of physical interception combined with supply chain efficiency, though the role of AI is implicit rather than explicit.
