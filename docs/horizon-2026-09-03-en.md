### [AI Speeds Up Research but Makes True Needs Harder to Find](https://www.woshipm.com/ai/6458294.html) ⭐️ 7.0/10

Li Wei, founder of Aquria.AI, argues that while AI accelerates user research by expanding reach and speed, it also complicates the validation of true user needs because AI-generated content can appear convincingly real. He presents methods such as AI-driven probing of real user behavior via screen recordings, cross-lingual interviews that preserve local context, and building evidence chains to trace conclusions back to raw data. In a case study with a Southeast Asian robot vacuum brand, AI interviews with over 1,000 users in two days revealed that non-standard debris like branches and nutshells, not pet hair, was a key purchase concern, validated by over 300 users mentioning similar issues. This matters for growth practitioners because it shows how AI can combine scale and depth in research, but emphasizes that human judgment and evidence retention are critical to avoid being misled by plausible AI outputs.

rss · 人人都是产品经理 · Sep 3, 00:59

**「AI Technique」** The approach uses AI to conduct dynamic, conversational interviews at scale, where the AI asks follow-up questions based on each user&\#x27;s responses and observed behavior \(e.g., screen recordings\). It also employs multilingual AI to interview users in their local language and context, and builds evidence chains by tagging and storing raw data for traceability.

**「Growth Impact」** The robot vacuum case demonstrates how AI-driven research can uncover region-specific product requirements that directly influence purchase decisions, potentially improving product-market fit and conversion in new markets. The method also reduces the time and cost of international research by eliminating the need for local teams, enabling faster iteration and more informed product decisions.

**「Takeaway」** Growth practitioners should use AI to probe real user behavior and preserve evidence chains, ensuring that insights are validated by raw data and not just AI-generated summaries, to avoid acting on false needs.
