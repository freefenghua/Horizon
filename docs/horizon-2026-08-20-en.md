The article argues that AI-assisted sales, where AI generates responses but humans review and send them, merely optimizes existing workflows and leaves humans as the bottleneck. True AI-native sales requires redesigning the division of labor between humans and AI, delegating repetitive tasks like reception, follow-up, and needs assessment to AI, with humans only stepping in at critical closing moments. The author reports that in their own practice, AI handles about 80% of pre-sales work. They propose two key standards for effective AI sales: customers are willing to continue chatting even when they know it&\#x27;s AI, and AI genuinely takes over a significant portion of sales work. The article emphasizes that the real issue is not whether AI replies automatically, but whether AI can actually sell, avoiding mechanical conversation patterns that feel &\#x27;too AI&\#x27;.

### [Why Big Tech Is Abandoning MCP: Token Waste and Complexity](https://www.woshipm.com/ai/5973563.html) ⭐️ 7.0/10

The article explains why MCP \(Model Context Protocol\) is losing favor to CLI+API for AI agent development, citing a Scalekit benchmark showing up to 32x token waste with MCP. Industry figures like Perplexity&\#x27;s CTO and Y Combinator&\#x27;s core team have publicly shifted to CLI+API. The main drawbacks are severe token waste, architectural redundancy, and security vulnerabilities. However, MCP is not obsolete; the selection depends on scale: CLI for small-scale efficiency, MCP for large-scale standardization. The article provides practical guidance for developers to choose the right tool.

rss · 人人都是产品经理 · Aug 19, 06:29

**「AI Technique」** The article discusses MCP, a protocol for standardizing AI tool calls, and CLI \(command-line interface\) as an alternative. MCP loads full tool definitions into the model context, causing token waste, while CLI calls tools directly with minimal overhead.

**「Growth Impact」** The article reports that MCP can cost up to 32x more tokens than CLI, with a concrete example: a simple task uses 1,365 tokens with CLI vs 44,026 with MCP. For 10,000 operations, MCP costs about $55/month vs $3 for CLI, significantly impacting operational costs at scale.

**「Takeaway」** For growth practitioners, choose CLI for simple, high-efficiency tasks to reduce token costs and latency, but use MCP for multi-tenant, compliance-heavy scenarios where audit trails and user isolation are critical.
