### [Submagic: AI Captions + 30% Affiliate Drive $8M ARR](https://www.woshipm.com/chuangye/6478955.html) ⭐️ 7.0/10

Submagic, a French AI video-captioning tool, reached $8M ARR by June 2025 with only 13 employees and no external funding, according to co-founder and CEO David Zitoun. The product automatically generates dynamic subtitles, highlights keywords, removes filler pauses, and adds b-roll, addressing the repetitive editing work that short-video creators face daily. It hit $1M ARR just three months after its first paying customer in May 2023, and now attracts roughly 5,000–10,000 new signups per day. Growth is driven by a 30% recurring affiliate commission that has attracted over 10,000 promoters, who generate about 20% of revenue, though the company also discloses roughly 15% monthly paid churn. For growth practitioners, this is a concrete case of using product-visible output as distribution and aligning affiliate payouts with recurring revenue rather than one-time acquisition.

rss · 人人都是产品经理 · Oct 16, 02:36

**「AI Technique」** Submagic uses speech recognition and generative AI to auto-generate synchronized dynamic captions, detect and remove filler pauses and verbal slips, and recommend or insert b-roll based on video content. It later added Magic Clips, which automatically extracts short social-ready clips from longer videos.

**「Growth Impact」** The affiliate program pays 30% recurring commission, so promoters earn as long as referred customers keep paying; by June 2025, over 10,000 partners drove about 20% of revenue, with estimated monthly commission payouts of $40,000–$45,000. Despite roughly 15% monthly paid churn, Submagic sustained growth through high new signups and higher-priced plans, whose churn is reportedly lower than individual users.

**「Takeaway」** If your product&\#x27;s output is publicly visible, turn users into affiliates with recurring commissions so their content keeps selling while they keep earning, but pair it with a plan to reduce churn by moving users toward higher-frequency, team-based use cases.
