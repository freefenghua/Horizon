For growth practitioners, the key signal is that pricing units, not model quality, now separate Chinese and overseas teams: one sells monthly memberships, the other sells enterprise seats and per-million-character API calls.

### [Fresh Grocery Chain Reframes AI Customer Service as Intent-Routing Assistant](https://www.woshipm.com/ai/6474119.html) ⭐️ 5.0/10

A small community fresh-grocery chain \(serving 80–160 customers per store\) describes how it reframed an &\#x27;AI customer service&\#x27; project into a &\#x27;self-service routing assistant&\#x27; after analyzing six months of customer inquiries. The team found that query-type questions \(order status, delivery timing, address changes\) made up more than half of inquiries, while pre-purchase product questions were scattered and specialized, making them a conversion lever rather than a post-sale cost. The core module is intent recognition, positioned as a routing layer rather than a processing layer, using a six-step pipeline \(preprocessing, keyword rule screening, vector classifier, routing table lookup, slot pre-fetching, and fallback\) and a three-tier cascade \(keyword rules, vector classifier, LLM fallback\). The author explicitly defers routing layers and effect data to a follow-up post, so no quantitative results are reported in this excerpt. For growth practitioners, the case illustrates how to scope an AI project around conversion-critical moments and design a cost-efficient intent-routing architecture.

rss · 人人都是产品经理 · Oct 7, 02:21

**「AI Technique」** The system uses a three-tier cascade for intent recognition: weighted keyword dictionaries for fast rule-based screening, a vector classifier \(embedding-based\) as the main workhorse, and a large language model with few-shot examples only for low-confidence or compound intents. The vector classifier is trained on 200–500 labeled examples per intent, converting sentences into embeddings and drawing decision boundaries in vector space.

**「Growth Impact」** The author states the goal is to reduce manual customer service connection rate by 40–50%, but no actual measured results are provided in this excerpt; effect data is deferred to a follow-up post. The mechanism is that pre-purchase product Q&amp;A is treated as a conversion lever—answering professionally can lead to immediate purchase, while failing to answer sends users to competitors.

**「Takeaway」** Before building an AI assistant, analyze historical customer inquiries to identify which question types are conversion-critical versus cost centers, and design intent recognition as a routing layer with a cost-efficient cascade \(rules → small model → LLM\) rather than defaulting to an LLM for every message.
