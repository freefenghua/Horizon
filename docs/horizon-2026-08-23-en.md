### [AI Consultant Advises Client Against Knowledge Base, Focuses on Problem Diagnosis](https://www.woshipm.com/ai/6452185.html) ⭐️ 7.0/10

An AI transformation consultant, Shen Yue, advised a leading Chinese OTA company against building a 200,000-document knowledge base, despite the client&\#x27;s initial enthusiasm. Instead of proposing a solution, Shen used four reasons to challenge the project: lack of maintenance, model hallucination risks, knowledge modeling difficulties, and absence of high-frequency use cases. The client, after reflection, narrowed the scope from company-wide to a specific user group and a smaller scenario. This case highlights the value of FDE \(Forward-Deployed Engineer\) in diagnosing real problems before solution design, emphasizing that problem identification is more critical than technical execution. The article lacks concrete metrics but offers a replicable framework for evaluating AI projects.

The technique discussed includes knowledge base construction, vector retrieval, and knowledge graphs, but the core insight is the importance of problem diagnosis over technical implementation.

**「Growth Impact」** The article does not provide quantitative growth metrics, but the qualitative impact is significant: by avoiding a doomed project, the client saved resources and redirected focus to a more viable, smaller-scale AI application. This aligns with growth principles of prioritizing high-impact, low-risk initiatives.

**「Takeaway」** Before starting any AI project, ask three questions: Who will use it daily? Who maintains it? What is the smallest scenario to pilot? If you can&\#x27;t answer, don&\#x27;t proceed.
