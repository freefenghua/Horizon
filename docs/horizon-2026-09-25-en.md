### [Midjourney: 11 People, Zero Funding, $200M Revenue — Aesthetics as the Growth Engine](https://www.woshipm.com/chuangye/6469811.html) ⭐️ 6.0/10

This case study examines how Midjourney, launched in July 2022 with an 11-person team, no external funding, no sales team, and no marketing budget, reached over 16 million users and more than $200 million in annual revenue by 2023, with a reported valuation exceeding $10 billion by 2025. Rather than competing with OpenAI&\#x27;s DALL-E, Stability AI&\#x27;s Stable Diffusion, Adobe&\#x27;s Firefly, or Google&\#x27;s Imagen on model accuracy, Midjourney treated aesthetics — lighting, composition, color, and style — as its core product metric, betting that users care about output quality rather than technical parameters. It also ran entirely inside Discord instead of building a standalone app, adopted a subscription model \($10–$120 per month\) instead of free access, and designed its community around public-by-default generation, shared prompts, and leaderboards. The article is a strategic framing piece rather than a controlled growth experiment: the metrics cited are company-level outcomes, and the source does not provide before/after conversion or retention data, so the causal claims should be treated as the author&\#x27;s interpretation rather than verified causal evidence.

**「AI Technique」** Midjourney&\#x27;s team spent significant effort tuning the model&\#x27;s aesthetic output — light, composition, color, and style — rather than optimizing purely for prompt accuracy, positioning the model as &\#x27;not the most advanced&\#x27; but producing the &\#x27;best-looking&\#x27; results. **「Takeaway」** When building an AI product, optimize the quality of the output users actually share — not the model&\#x27;s technical benchmark — and consider whether an existing platform&\#x27;s community can serve as your product interface instead of building one from scratch.

### [Tencent QClaw Shutdown: The AI Agent Demo-to-Retention Gap](https://www.woshipm.com/ai/6469810.html) ⭐️ 6.0/10

Tencent&\#x27;s QClaw, an AI office agent launched in March 2026 with low-barrier installation and WeChat-based remote computer control as its core selling points, announced on September 24, 2026 that it will officially cease operations on December 24, 2026, citing &quot;business development adjustments.&quot; The product rode the OpenClaw hype wave from late January 2026, but by July 2026 reports indicated QClaw&\#x27;s business and some team members were being reassigned to the WorkBuddy department, and the shutdown notice followed in September. A survey by Growth Black Box and NetEase Zhioi \(April 2026, 2,000 individual respondents and 100 enterprise managers\) found that among 999 actual or former users, 309 had stopped using the product—nearly one-third—and about two-thirds of those who quit did so within the first two weeks. The case illustrates the retention gap between AI agent demos and sustained usage, and the strategic logic of big-company entry-point consolidation, though the source provides no retention, conversion, or DAU figures for QClaw itself.

rss · 人人都是产品经理 · Sep 24, 08:19

**「AI Technique」** QClaw is a desktop AI agent that lets users control their computer remotely via WeChat messages, using low-barrier installation as its main differentiator. The underlying agent technology plans and calls tools and models to execute tasks on the user&\#x27;s machine, which the article notes consumes more tokens than simpler AI products.

**「Growth Impact」** The article reports no QClaw-specific retention, conversion, or DAU metrics. The only quantitative data comes from a broader OpenClaw survey: among 999 actual or former users, 309 \(nearly one-third\) had stopped using the product, and roughly two-thirds of those quit within the first two weeks. The mechanism described is that novelty and curiosity drive initial adoption, but sustained usage requires a recurring task that the agent handles more conveniently than the user can—a need the article says is an order of magnitude smaller than the novelty-driven audience.

**「Takeaway」** When evaluating or building an AI agent product, distinguish between novelty-driven adoption and recurring-task-driven retention: the former can create viral growth, but only the latter sustains usage, so validate whether users have a repeated task the agent genuinely simplifies before scaling acquisition.
